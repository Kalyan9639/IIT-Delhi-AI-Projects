🚀 Project Overview
Poll Intelligence & Market Insight System

This project is not just a “visualizer.” It is a data-driven decision support system that transforms raw survey responses into actionable business insights.

You will build a system that:

Processes real survey data
Identifies patterns in customer preferences
Segments users (demographics, regions, etc.)
Generates insights that can guide product, marketing, or strategy decisions
🧠 Simple Explanation

You are building a tool that answers:

“What do people prefer, who prefers it, and what should we do about it?”

Instead of just showing charts, your system will:

Detect trends
Highlight key segments
Suggest decisions
⚙️ Technical Explanation

The system performs:

Data Ingestion
Load structured survey data (CompleteResponses.csv)
Data Processing
Clean missing/inconsistent values
Standardize categories
Analytical Engine
Compute distributions (percentages, counts)
Segment data (age, region, etc.)
Compare options
Insight Engine
Identify dominant preferences
Detect weak/strong segments
Generate rule-based insights
Visualization Layer
Bar charts, pie charts, trend graphs
Optional dashboard (Streamlit)
🔄 End-to-End Workflow
Survey Data (CSV)
        ↓
Data Cleaning & Preprocessing
        ↓
Segmentation (age, region, etc.)
        ↓
Aggregation (counts, % share)
        ↓
Comparative Analysis
        ↓
Insight Generation
        ↓
Visualization / Dashboard
        ↓
Business Decisions
