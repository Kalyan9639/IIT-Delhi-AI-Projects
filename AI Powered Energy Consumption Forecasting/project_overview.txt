Problem: People and businesses often pay more because they don’t know how their energy usage
fluctuates.
Solution: The AI system helps forecast and control energy usage to avoid peak-time penalties.

Environmental Impact &amp; Carbon Emissions

carbon emissions.
Solution: Forecasting allows better planning, helping cities move towards sustainable energy usage
and net-zero goals.

Manual Monitoring &amp; Inefficiency
Problem: Traditional monitoring of energy consumption is manual, slow, and error-prone.
Solution: AI automates predictions and analysis in real-time, reducing human error and response
delays.

Mismatch Between Production &amp; Consumption
Problem: Power plants often generate more or less electricity than needed, wasting resources.
Solution: Your forecasting system bridges the gap, helping producers align with real-time demand.

Lack of AI Talent in Clean Tech Sector

Solution: By learning this project, you are becoming part of a rare, high-demand talent pool for
smart grids, green buildings, and climate tech.

## Steps to perform:

Step 1: Build an AI That Predicts Future Power Demand
What are we doing?
We’re building an AI system that can predict how much electricity will be used hourly/daily in the
future.
Why are we doing it?
To help smart buildings, factories, and cities optimize usage, reduce wastage, and avoid blackouts.


Step 2: Teach Your AI with Real-World Smart Grid Logs
What are we doing?
Loading and visualizing real historical energy usage data.
Why are we doing it?

To allow AI to learn from daily and hourly consumption patterns and prepare the data in a format it
can learn from.


Step 3: Let Your AI Learn from Human Behavior (Time-Based Patterns)
What are we doing?
Extracting hour and day of week features from the dataset.
Why are we doing it?
To teach AI how energy usage changes with human routines (e.g., office hours, weekends).


Step 4: Build an Intelligent  Network to Forecast Energy Usage
What are we doing?
Training a XGBoost or RandomForest to forecast electricity usage.
Why are we doing it?
To build a smart AI that understands complex energy usage patterns.


Step 5: Freeze the Brain of Your AI — Save the Learned Intelligence
What are we doing?
Saving the trained model so it can be used again without retraining.
Why are we doing it?
To reuse the model instantly for real-time prediction systems.


Step 6: Give Your AI a Voice — Launch a Real-Time Prediction Engine
What are we doing?
Creating a FastAPI API that returns energy usage predictions for any hour and day.
Why are we doing it?
To simulate how apps or systems will call your AI to get instant results.
