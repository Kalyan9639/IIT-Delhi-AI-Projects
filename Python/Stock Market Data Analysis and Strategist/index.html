User Guide

1. The Market Pulse (Bell Curve)

This shows how TCS is moving today compared to the last 5 days. If the Red Dot is in the center, the market is stable. If it moves to the extreme edges, something unusual is happening.

2. Macro Sensors

We monitor Indian Government data (MOSPI). High inflation (CPI > 6%) or high Fuel costs usually cause stock prices to drop later. Red icons mean high risk.

3. Cognitive Analysis

Our Cloud AI reads the headlines and links them to the math. It tells you exactly "What is happening" and "What to do" in plain English, so you don't have to be a finance expert.
