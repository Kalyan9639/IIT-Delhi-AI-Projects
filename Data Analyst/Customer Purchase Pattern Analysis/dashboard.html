Insights & Recommendations

Optimize Peak Hours: Order volume peaks mid-week during specific morning/afternoon windows. Align marketing "flash sales" and customer support staffing to these high-traffic periods to maximize conversion and engagement.

RFM-Driven Personalization: High-value RFM segments ("Champions") show distinct monetary spend behavior. Create exclusive "VIP early access" programs for these top-tier customers to solidify loyalty and boost lifetime value.

Operational Risk Mitigation: Countries with high cancellation rates warrant an immediate logistics audit. Investigate shipping delays or regional customs issues for these specific locations to reduce future returns and improve overall customer experience.

Basket Optimization: Customers show a preference for specific basket sizes on weekends. Utilize cross-selling algorithms to recommend complementary items to customers whose basket size is just below your average weekend target.
